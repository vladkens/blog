Hi, I'm Vlad. This is my personal blog. I write here about what I find interesting: code, tools, things I tried and how they went, and sometimes a look back at the year.

I also make open-source projects. Most of my time right now goes to [Wavo](/apps/wavo/), local dictation: hold a key, speak, and the text lands where you were typing. Two older ones have a life of their own: [macmon](/apps/macmon/), a monitor for Apple Silicon Macs, and [twscrape](/apps/twscrape/), a library for collecting data from X. The rest is on the [apps page](/apps/).

If you want to ask something or just say hi, write to [hello@vladkens.me](mailto:hello@vladkens.me), or find me on [X](https://x.com/uid127) and [GitHub](https://github.com/vladkens).
